# Cordonnier Software

**Cordonnier** — French for *shoemaker*, the trade of four generations of the LeFort family — is the home of the software built by Walter LeFort in Sutton, Massachusetts.

The work here follows a few habits: self-hosted where it can be, evidence over assumption, and built to solve a real problem in front of the builder — a family tree that shows its sources, a parts lookup that knows what fits, a tracker that keeps a gig worker's numbers honest.

## Projects

### Genealogy & records

- **[Apex Family Tree](https://github.com/Cordonnier-Software/Apex-Family-Tree)** — Self-hosted, evidence-first genealogy. People, families, events, places, photographs, documents, and citations — every conclusion in the tree traceable back to the record it came from.

### Home inventory — the Nestarr suite

- **[Nestarr](https://github.com/Cordonnier-Software/Nestarr)** — A home inventory system: know what you own, where it is, and what it's worth. Live at [nestarr.com](https://nestarr.com).
- **[Android-Nestarr](https://github.com/Cordonnier-Software/Android-Nestarr)** — The Android companion app for the inventory.
- **[HA-Nestarr](https://github.com/Cordonnier-Software/HA-Nestarr)** — The Home Assistant integration for Nestarr.

### Gig work

- **[GigTracker](https://github.com/Cordonnier-Software/GigTracker)** — An all-in-one application to track gig work: earnings, mileage, and expenses, kept dash by dash instead of reconstructed at tax time. Live at [gigtracker.work](https://gigtracker.work).

### RV life

- **[RV_Interchange](https://github.com/Cordonnier-Software/RV_Interchange)** — An app specializing in RV parts and sales: Hollander-style interchange lookup, so you can cross-reference what fits what before you buy. Live at [rvinterchange.com](https://rvinterchange.com).
- **[RVLevel](https://github.com/Cordonnier-Software/RVLevel)** — A simple Android RV leveling system for getting a parked rig flat and settled.

### Home & energy

- **[Oil_Tank_Sensor](https://github.com/Cordonnier-Software/Oil_Tank_Sensor)** — The oil tank sensor project: heating-oil tank level monitoring, so the gauge in the basement isn't the only warning you get.

### Yard & community

- **[Apex Plow](https://github.com/Cordonnier-Software/ApexPlow)** — Snow-plow work, organized: the winter counterpart to the rest of the toolkit.
- **[Apex Scout Manager](https://github.com/Cordonnier-Software/Apex-Scout-Manager)** — A Scouting management tool: rosters, advancement, and the paperwork that comes with both.

### Games

- **[CivicDeNovo](https://github.com/Cordonnier-Software/CivicDeNovo)** — A virtual governance game: build a government from scratch — *de novo* — and run it.
- **[SeaQuacks](https://github.com/Cordonnier-Software/SeaQuacks)** — A cruise duck hunt game. Yes, the rubber ducks people hide on cruise ships. Hunt them.

### Also

- **[CivicMirror](https://github.com/CivicMirror)** — In its own organization: a civic-data platform that normalizes election data into a unified API. Live at [civicmirror.app](https://civicmirror.app).
